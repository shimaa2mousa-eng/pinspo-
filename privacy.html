Privacy Policy

Last updated: November 21, 2026

The short version: Pinspo has no accounts, no ads and no tracking. Your boards and photos stay in your own browser. They are never sent to us.

What Pinspo is

Pinspo (pinspo.org) is a free mood board website. You add pictures, notes and decorations to a cork board and can save the result as a picture.

What we collect

We don't collect any personal information. There is no sign-up, login, form or database, and we don't use cookies, analytics or advertising tools.

What is stored on your device

To remember your work, Pinspo saves a few things in your browser's local storage on your own device:

  • Your board (pinspoBoard): what you placed on it, where, and any text you typed. Photos you upload are resized and stored here too.
  • Your sound setting (pinspoSound): whether the click sound is on or off.

This information never leaves your device, and we can't see it. It stays until you clear your browser's site data or use the "Clear entire board" button. A different browser or device will not have your board, so use "Save as picture" if you want to keep a copy.

Photos and saved pictures

Pictures you upload are processed inside your browser only. When you save your board as a picture, that image is created on your device and downloaded by you. It is not uploaded anywhere.

Other companies that help the site work

Pinspo is a simple website, and it relies on a few services. When you open the site, your browser connects to them and they may receive standard technical information such as your IP address and browser type. We don't control or receive that information, and each company handles it under its own policy.

  • GitHub Pages hosts the site. GitHub Privacy Statement
  • Google Fonts supplies the handwriting fonts. Google Privacy Policy
  • cdnjs (Cloudflare) delivers the code that lets you save your board as a picture. Cloudflare Privacy Policy
  • Cloudflare also manages the pinspo.org domain name.

Children

Pinspo is not directed at children under 13, and we don't knowingly collect information from anyone. Because the site collects no personal information, there is nothing to collect from a child either.

Changes to this policy

If Pinspo adds features that change how information is handled, such as accounts, payments or analytics, this page will be updated first, and the date at the top will change.

Questions

You can reach out by opening an issue on the Pinspo project page on GitHub.
